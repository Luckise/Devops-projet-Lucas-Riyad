# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People looking to discover and attend local events, manage their upcoming schedule, and access their purchased tickets. They are likely using the app on-the-go to quickly check event details or show a ticket at the door.

## Product Purpose

To provide a seamless, engaging platform for discovering recent events (feed), browsing upcoming events, handling the registration handoff (via HelloAsso), and storing access tickets. Success means users can easily find something to do and flawlessly retrieve their ticket when they arrive.

## Brand Personality

Vibrant, enthusiastic, community-focused, and welcoming. It should feel alive and active, reflecting the energy of the events it hosts.

## References

- Reference: Fever (image-forward, curated feel, highly visual, easy browsing)
- Anti-reference: Cluttered, generic event listing sites (like basic Eventbrite templates) that feel like spreadsheets rather than experiences.

## Design Principles

- **Visuals lead the way**: Let event imagery do the heavy lifting for engagement.
- **Clear handoffs**: The transition to HelloAsso for payment/joining should feel intentional and trustworthy.
- **Glanceable utility**: The ticket/upcoming views must prioritize immediate readability (QR codes, times, locations) over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA compliance. Ensure high contrast for essential event details (time, location) even when overlaid on vibrant imagery.
